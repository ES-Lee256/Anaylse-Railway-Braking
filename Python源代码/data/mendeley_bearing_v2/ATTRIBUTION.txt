Dataset: Bearing Vibration Data under Time-varying Rotational Speed Conditions
DOI: https://doi.org/10.17632/v43hmbwxpm.2
Authors: Huan Huang; Natalie Baddour
Institution: University of Ottawa
Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/v43hmbwxpm/2
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This project includes 15 selected raw MATLAB files from the dataset:
H-A-1/2/3, I-A-1/2/3, O-A-1/2/3, B-A-1/2/3, and C-A-1/2/3.
They cover increasing rotational speed trials for five bearing conditions.
The files are unmodified. The software's preprocessing and derived model are
changes made for educational use; the dataset authors do not endorse this use.
